# South Sudan - States and Administrative Areas
**Source:** [Wikipedia - States of South Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_South_Sudan)

## Overview
- **Total States:** 10
- **Administrative Areas:** 3 (Abyei Area, Greater Pibor Area, Ruweng Area)
- **Total Population (2010):** ~8,923,553
- **Total Area:** ~644,329 km²
- **National Capital:** Juba (Central Equatoria)
